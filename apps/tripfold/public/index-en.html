FAQ

Is it free?Yes. Importing bookings, the plan, maps, cost splitting and editing with one other person are free.
Where do my booking emails go?Reading happens on your iPhone. Only trips you share for co-editing are sent to the server to sync.
Do I need an account?No. Install and start right away.
What is shown when I publish a plan?Only places, day numbers, times and from/to for transport. Booking codes, prices, notes, addresses, photos and companions are never published.
