A weekend in Paris: two days, no rush

PR: Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you book through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Prices and timetables change; check the official sites before you travel.

Two days is enough for a first taste of Paris if you book the big museums in advance and walk between neighbouring sights.

Day 1 — The historic centre

Book a morning slot at the Louvre, then walk through the Tuileries. Cross to Île de la Cité for Notre-Dame and Sainte-Chapelle, and spend the evening in the Latin Quarter.

Day 2 — Montmartre, the Marais and the Eiffel Tower

Climb to Sacré-Cœur and wander Montmartre in the morning. After lunch explore the Marais and Place des Vosges, then see the Eiffel Tower sparkle on the hour after dark from Trocadéro.

Tips
  • Timed tickets for the Louvre and the Eiffel Tower sell out at busy times; book a few weeks ahead.
  • Many museums close one day a week; check before you plan.
  • The metro is the fastest way to cross the city.
