How to keep all your travel bookings in one place

PR: Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you book through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Prices and timetables change; check the official sites before you travel.

Confirmation emails, PDFs, wallet passes and screenshots end up in five different apps. Here is a simple way to keep everything in one timeline.

1. Collect every booking as soon as you make it

Forward or save each confirmation right away: flights, hotels, trains, car hire, tours and restaurant reservations. Note the booking reference and the cancellation deadline.

2. Put everything on one timeline in local time

Time zones are the most common source of mistakes. Store each time in the local time of the place it happens, and check overnight flights that land on the next day.

3. Keep offline copies of tickets

Save QR codes and boarding passes on your phone so they open without signal at the gate or the station.

4. Share one plan with your travel group

One shared plan avoids "which hotel are we in?" messages and makes it easy to split costs at the end.

5. Check for gaps

Look for nights with no hotel, cities with no transport between them, and late arrivals with no transfer. That is when last-minute bookings get expensive.
