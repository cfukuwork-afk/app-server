London in 3 days: a balanced first visit

London's big museums are free, which makes it easy to mix famous sights with relaxed time. This plan groups each day along the river or around one area.

Day 1 — Westminster and the South Bank

Start at Westminster Abbey and the Houses of Parliament, walk past Downing Street to Trafalgar Square and the National Gallery. Cross to the South Bank for the London Eye, then walk east along the river to Tate Modern, Shakespeare's Globe and Borough Market.

Day 2 — The City and the East

Visit the Tower of London early, cross Tower Bridge, then walk to St Paul's Cathedral. In the afternoon head to Shoreditch for street art and Brick Lane for food.

Day 3 — Museums and parks

Spend the morning at the British Museum, or South Kensington for the Natural History Museum and the V&A. Walk through Hyde Park, then finish in Covent Garden or with a West End show.

Getting around
  • Tap in and out with a contactless card or phone; daily fares are capped automatically.
  • Buses are a cheap way to sightsee; they don't take cash.
  • Many attractions are cheaper when booked online in advance.
