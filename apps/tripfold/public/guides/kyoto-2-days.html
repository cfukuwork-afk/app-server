Kyoto in 2 days: temples, gardens and food

Kyoto rewards early starts. The big sights are busiest from late morning, so plan the famous places first and keep afternoons for walking and food.

Day 1 — Fushimi Inari and Higashiyama

Go to Fushimi Inari Taisha at sunrise; the gates are quiet and the light is best. Climb as far as you like — the crowds thin out after the first viewpoint.

Take the train back towards the centre and walk the Higashiyama district: Kiyomizu-dera, the Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka lanes, Yasaka Shrine and, in the evening, the streets of Gion.

Day 2 — Arashiyama and central Kyoto

Head west to Arashiyama for the bamboo grove (again, early), Tenryu-ji's garden and the Togetsukyo bridge. In the afternoon visit Kinkaku-ji (the Golden Pavilion), then finish at Nishiki Market for street food.

Tips
  • Buses get crowded; combine trains with walking where you can.
  • Many temples close around 17:00. Check opening hours the day before.
  • If you are coming from Tokyo, the shinkansen takes a little over two hours.
