Heathrow to central London: every option compared

Heathrow has four good ways into the city. The best one depends on where you are staying, how much luggage you have and when you land.

Elizabeth line

The best all-rounder. It runs from Heathrow through Paddington, Bond Street, Tottenham Court Road and on to the City and Canary Wharf without changes. Pay with a contactless card or phone.

Heathrow Express

The fastest non-stop link to Paddington (about 15 minutes). It costs more than the Elizabeth line unless you book early, so it makes most sense if you are staying near Paddington and are short on time.

Piccadilly line (Underground)

The cheapest rail option and a direct ride to Kensington, Knightsbridge, Piccadilly Circus and King's Cross. It is slower and has stairs at some stations, which matters with big suitcases.

Coach and taxi

National Express coaches go to Victoria Coach Station. Black cabs and pre-booked cars are door to door; a pre-booked transfer is usually cheaper than a cab from the rank and handy late at night.

Quick pick
  • Staying in the West End or the City: Elizabeth line.
  • Staying at Paddington and in a hurry: Heathrow Express.
  • On a budget with light bags: Piccadilly line.
  • Arriving late, with family or lots of luggage: pre-booked transfer.
