How to split travel costs with friends without awkwardness

Money is the most common cause of friction on group trips. A few simple rules keep things fair and quick to settle.

Agree on the rules before you go

  • Which costs are shared (accommodation, transport, group meals) and which are personal.
  • Whether to split equally or by usage, for example when someone skips a dinner.

Record each expense when it happens

Write down who paid, how much, in which currency and who it was for. Photos of receipts help later.

Handle multiple currencies

Record the original amount and currency, then convert at a single agreed rate when you settle, or at the rate on the day each expense was paid.

Settle with the fewest transfers

Instead of everyone paying everyone, calculate net balances and have each person who owes pay the people who are owed. In a group of four this usually means two or three transfers instead of a dozen.
